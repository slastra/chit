chit privacy policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

chit is a notes and checklists app for a household. Its server is run by Shaun Lastra at chit.lastra.us for people he invites. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored

  • Your account: your name, email address and password (stored hashed).
  • What you write: notes, checklists, labels, reminders, and the files you attach (pictures, voice memos you record, documents).
  • Who you share with: which notes you share, with whom, and their role.

The app keeps a copy of your notes on your device so it works offline. The server keeps the same notes so your devices and the people you share with stay in step.

What it is used for

Only to run chit: showing your notes on your devices and to the people you share them with. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you record a voice memo.
  • Notifications and alarms: to remind you at the time you set, and to tell you when someone edits a note shared with you.
  • Camera and files: only when you choose to attach a picture or file.

Who can see your notes

You, the people you share a note with, and the server's administrator, who can reach the server's storage. Data travels over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note forever removes it from the server and every device. To delete your account and everything in it, ask the administrator at shaun@lastra.us.
